      How to Play

      • 👆 Drag your finger or mouse to move the bucket.
      • Catch blue drops. 10 fill a bucket.
      • Brown drops contaminate the bucket.
      • Bump the filter into brown drops to make them blue.
      • Filter a glowing green drop for 2 bonus blue drops.
      • Dodge trash. 3 hits break your bucket and cost a life. Trash also breaks the filter, and you get it back when you fill a bucket.
      • 💧💧💧 Lose all your lives and the game ends. Fill buckets fast: your score is buckets per minute.
